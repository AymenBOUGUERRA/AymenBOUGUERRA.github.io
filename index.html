NeurIPS 2026 · Main Conference Paper

Disentangling the Good From the Bad: Quantization-Induced Flips Are Not Random

Aymen Bouguerra, Fabio Arnez, Alexandra Gomez-Villa, Chokri Mraidha

Good and bad prediction flips separate along the SVD rank of the quantized model's own feature space, so a post-hoc gate can keep the good flips and repair the bad ones.

ICML 2026 · Main Conference Paper

Less Precise Can Be More Reliable: A Systematic Evaluation of Quantization's Impact on VLMs Beyond Accuracy

Aymen Bouguerra, Daniel Alfonso Montoya Vasquez, Alexandra Gomez-Villa, Chokri Mraidha, Fabio Arnez

A large-scale evaluation of CLIP-family VLM quantization beyond accuracy: in specific regimes, quantization improves accuracy, calibration, OOD detection and noise robustness at once.

IEEE ICVES 2026 · Main Conference Paper

Quantization and Corruption Robustness in Deployed Road-Scene Perception

Aymen Bouguerra, Ansgar Radermacher, Fabio Arnez, Chokri Mraidha

On real adverse-weather driving images, 8-bit quantization costs a road-scene detector no blur robustness and makes a recognition encoder more robust to blur it never saw, while both run faster and smaller as true INT8 models.
